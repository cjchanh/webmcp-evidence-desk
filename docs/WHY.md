# Why Evidence Desk exists

High-stakes decisions force a bad choice: make a person read every document, or let an agent act and trust an opaque summary.

Evidence Desk is a third path — the agent does the reading, the person keeps the verdict, and a sealed record makes the whole exchange checkable after the fact. The pattern generalizes to legal, procurement, compliance, and anywhere an agent's conclusion needs to survive scrutiny.

The page puts the investigation in the open: the agent searches, inspects, and evaluates signed evidence, then stops. A person adjudicates. A tamper-evident record preserves both sides of that decision.
